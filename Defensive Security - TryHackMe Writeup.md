# Defensive Security - TryHackMe Writeup

## Overview

The **Defensive Security** room introduces the principles and responsibilities of protecting computer systems, networks, and data against cyber threats. It explains how cybersecurity professionals detect malicious activity, respond to security incidents, and continuously strengthen an organization's security posture.

Unlike offensive security, which focuses on discovering vulnerabilities through simulated attacks, defensive security emphasizes monitoring, detection, prevention, and incident response.

# Core Concepts

## What is Defensive Security?

Defensive Security is the practice of protecting systems, applications, and networks from cyber threats by preventing attacks, detecting malicious activity, and responding to security incidents.

The primary objective is to reduce risk, maintain system availability, and protect organizational assets.

---

## Blue Team

A Blue Team is responsible for defending an organization's infrastructure against cyber threats.

Typical responsibilities include:

* Monitoring systems
* Detecting attacks
* Investigating suspicious activity
* Responding to incidents
* Improving security controls
* Reducing future risks

Blue Teams work continuously to identify threats before they impact business operations.

---

## Security Operations Center (SOC)

A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a centralized team responsible for monitoring and defending an organization's information systems.

SOC analysts continuously monitor alerts, investigate suspicious behavior, and coordinate responses to security incidents.

Their work helps organizations quickly detect and contain cyberattacks.

---

## Incident Response

Incident Response is the structured process of handling cybersecurity incidents.

The typical lifecycle includes:

* Preparation
* Detection
* Analysis
* Containment
* Eradication
* Recovery
* Lessons Learned

Following a structured process minimizes damage and improves future security.

# Key Lessons Learned

## 1. Detection is Continuous

Cybersecurity is not a one-time activity.

Organizations must continuously monitor systems for suspicious activity and respond quickly to emerging threats.

---

## 2. Prevention Alone is Not Enough

Even with strong security controls, attacks may still occur.

Defensive teams must be prepared to detect, investigate, contain, and recover from security incidents.

---

## 3. Logs Are Valuable Sources of Evidence

Logs provide critical information during investigations.

Analyzing authentication records, system logs, and application logs enables defenders to reconstruct attacker activity and identify compromised systems.

---

## 4. Teamwork is Essential

Defensive Security involves collaboration between SOC analysts, incident responders, system administrators, and other IT professionals to protect organizational assets.
